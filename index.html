      How even is the 2027 schedule?

      Each person's work is compared with their fair share: the year's total for that kind of shift × their FTE ÷ everyone's FTE. Time away is made up on other dates, so it doesn't lower anyone's share. The shared position's weekend share is set by their agreed weekend structure. Balances carry over from 2026 (total call shifts) and the published months, so people who did more before are given less in this plan and vice versa; the tiles show how far anyone's running balance is from even by the end of the plan.

      % of share = how far someone is from their fair share, divided by that share. SD (standard deviation) = the typical distance from fair share across everyone; the smaller, the more even. Half-time physicians have smaller shares, so the same one-shift difference is a bigger percentage for them. Stat holiday shifts are so few (about 1–3 a year each) that being one shift off looks huge in percent; read those in shifts.

      Group members

      Add someone joining the group, or set a last day for someone leaving. Changes go out with the requests file and take effect at the next re-plan. A new person's share counts from their start date (prorated, like long leave in reverse) and their balance starts at 0. Someone leaving is scheduled up to their last day, and their past shifts stay in everyone's history; whatever they are over or under their share when they go is shared out among the rest of the group by FTE. The shared position's shared position and the cardiologist are set up separately.

      Contested weeks

      When more people ask to be away on weekdays of the same week than can be spared (everyone who takes call; the two people sharing one FTE count as half a person each, as they share one FTE), the week goes first to whoever has won the fewest contested weeks in the past 12 months (counting weeks won earlier this year), then to whoever submitted first, then by a recorded draw. Anyone who has already won the yearly limit goes last. Only weekday absences count (a stat Monday joined to the weekend before doesn't). Waitlisted weekdays go to the scheduler as "prefer off" rather than time away; weekend days stay as asked. Enter past wins below (none on record yet).

      Change who is on call

      Changes go on top of the published schedule; nobody else moves. When the scheduler re-reads the file it re-checks every rule and the fairness tallies, and flags anything a change breaks. A shift a locum takes from someone who ticked "Open to locums" still counts as theirs.

      Send to the scheduler

      Rebuilding the schedule: once the request window closes, download requests.json and run the scheduler on it (about 20 minutes). It rebuilds the whole year from everyone's latest requests. After the schedule is published, use "Change who is on call" instead, so one change doesn't reshuffle everyone. In the GitHub version this becomes a "Rebuild schedule" button here.

      Load files from the scheduler

      After publishing a month, load the scheduler's web_data.json here: everyone then sees the new published calendar and fairness figures. "Import requests" loads a requests.json (for example the old spreadsheet's requests) and keeps the times recorded in it; it replaces those people's requests.

      Sign-in accounts

      Two steps for each person. 1) In Supabase, Authentication › Users › Add user › Create new user: their email, a starting password, and tick "Auto Confirm User". 2) Here, link that email to their name. Only linked emails can see anything. Admins see everyone's requests and can change settings.
